OUR STORY

MORE THAN A HAIRCUT.

Toughcuts started as a single chair in a garage and grew into a studio built on one idea: every client leaves looking sharper than when they walked in. No rushed clippers, no guesswork — just barbers who treat every fade, every line-up, every beard trim like it's the only appointment of the day.

Years later, that idea hasn't changed. We've just gotten better at it.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

MISSION & VALUES.

The three things every cut is built on.

Precision

Every line, fade, and taper is measured, not guessed. Consistency is the whole craft.

Passion

Our barbers train off the clock because cutting hair well is something they actually care about.

Community

A barbershop is a neighborhood spot first. We know your name before we know your fade.

FROM THE FOUNDER

Klark Dizon

Owner & Master Barber · 10 Years

I didn't open Toughcuts to be the biggest shop in town. I opened it so every person who sits in the chair leaves looking like the best version of themselves — that's still the only standard that matters here.

Klark started cutting hair out of a one-chair garage setup and built Toughcuts from the ground up, one client at a time. Ten years later he still works the chair most days — hot towel shaves and traditional cuts are his specialty, and he trains every barber on the team personally before they touch a client.
