This page explains, in plain language, how Toughcuts uses information submitted through this website. It's a general notice and should be reviewed and customized by the business before launch.

01 Information we collect

When you create an account, book an appointment, or place an order, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, appointment details, delivery information, and order information.

02 How we use information

  • To manage accounts, appointments, orders, payments, and customer support.
  • To send service-related confirmations, updates, and delivery or pickup notices.
  • To maintain site security, prevent abuse, and improve the customer experience.

03 Sharing and retention

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the website, such as authentication, hosting, payment, and delivery providers. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes and applicable business obligations.

04 Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information by contacting the shop. Please don't submit sensitive information that the site doesn't request.

05 Contact

For privacy questions, contact Toughcuts through the contact details published on the website.
